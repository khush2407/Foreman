[1.6] *** LEAKAGE CHECK — most important step in this script ***
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Rows where OR(sub-types) == Machine failure: 9,973 / 10,000
  Mismatched rows: 27

  Breakdown of the mismatches:
TWF                 0
HDF                 0
PWF                 0
OSF                 0
RNF                18
Machine failure     9

  These are RNF (random failure) rows flagged 1 while 'Machine failure'
  stayed 0 — a documented quirk of this dataset. RNF is random noise by
  construction and carries no predictive signal.

  CONCLUSION: 'Machine failure' is essentially the OR of the sub-type
  flags. Feeding TWF/HDF/PWF/OSF/RNF into a model that predicts
  'Machine failure' would leak the answer and produce a fake ~100% score.
  ==> All five sub-type columns are DROPPED from the feature set.
      They are kept only for descriptive analysis of failure causes.
