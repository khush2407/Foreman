Key EDA findings for the report:
  1. Severe class imbalance (3.39%) — accuracy is a meaningless metric here.
  2. Heat dissipation + overstrain + power = 87% of all flagged failures.
  3. No single sensor separates the classes (all |Cohen's d| < 0.8) —
     which is exactly why a multivariate model is needed.
  4. Air and process temperature are collinear (r = 0.88) — their
     DIFFERENCE carries the real signal, not either one alone.
  5. Failures cluster outside a safe torque-speed power band.
  6. Overstrain sits above a tool-wear x torque threshold.
  --> Findings 4, 5 and 6 directly motivate the engineered features in Stage 4.
